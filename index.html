Windsor Avenue, Hahndorf, in the Adelaide Hills

Two cabins on a 40‑acre working farm.

Hideaway and Sunset each sleep two. Hahndorf's Main Street is about 4 km back down the road.

Follow the drive up the hill.

Each cabin sits on its own fenced paddock of about two acres, 60 metres from the other, with its own fire pit and picnic table.

Up the drive, a corten sign like the one at the gate points the way. Hideaway one way, Sunset the other.

The farm

Forty acres of paddock and gums, still worked. Cows graze the slopes, there's a koala in the gums, and a track runs out past the hay bales.

Your own fire pit and picnic table

On cooler nights, sit round the fire pit and roast marshmallows with a glass of local red. In summer, take lunch or a sunset dinner out to the picnic table instead.

There's no wifi in the cabins, on purpose, so you switch off. Phones get full 4G and 5G, and the smart TV is there if you want it.

Outdoor fires aren't allowed from 15 November to 1 May because of fire restrictions. Firewood for the indoor fire is A$25 a bag.
